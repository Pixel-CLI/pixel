**hooks:** `guard`, `prompt-submit`, `post-tool-use`, `metrics` and `task-event` read their payload through one bounded reader, `MAX_HOOK_INPUT` (1 MiB): an over-cap payload is refused after `cap + 1` bytes rather than allocated in full. The rest keep their fail-open exit 0; `task-event` has no silent path, so its over-cap input emits the unavailable response, denying `PreToolUse`. The cap bounds allocation, not the wait; a stalled pipe stays bounded by `pixel install`'s hook timeout.
