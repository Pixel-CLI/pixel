**mutants:** every mutation campaign (the pre-push gate, CI's shards and the nightly whole-tree run) now runs on a pinned nightly with libtest's `--fail-fast`: a caught mutant's suite stops at the first failing test instead of finishing every remaining one, roughly halving campaign time on a healthy suite. The flags live in `.cargo/mutants.toml` as every lane's shared program; the toolchain pin is installed by the workflows and sourced by the local lanes (`scripts/mutants-toolchain.sh`).
