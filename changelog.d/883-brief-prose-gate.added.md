**brief:** the prompt-submit brief now answers a prompt in plain language, English or French, when the repository's own vocabulary covers it, and stays silent otherwise, with no model in that decision. The daemon's `facts.relevance` and `meaning` ops pick the first `files:` and a `confidence:` line. Each decision is logged to `.pixel/brief-decisions.jsonl` (500 lines, `PIXEL_BRIEF_LOG=0` off) and `pixel brief --json` prints it.
