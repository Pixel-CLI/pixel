**brief:** the prompt-submit brief now answers a plain-language prompt, English first, when a fixed four-feature model finds the repository talks about it: a full brief with `confidence: high`, or a compact `confidence: low — possibly related` one of three files; nothing otherwise. The daemon's `facts.relevance` and `meaning` ops pick the files. Every decision is logged to `.pixel/brief-decisions.jsonl` (`PIXEL_BRIEF_LOG=0` off); `pixel brief --json` prints it.
