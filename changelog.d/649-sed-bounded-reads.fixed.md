**hooks:** the task-state hook classifies a `sed` script by its commands and substitution flags, so a script that writes or executes — `w`/`W`/`e` commands and `e`/`w`/`W` flags in any position, including `west`, `s/.*/x/ep`, inline `--expression=`/`-e`, and newline- or comment-carried forms — is denied while the ledger is unavailable, and bounded reads (`sed -n '433,472p'`) stay reads.
