**daemon, proto:** the `targets_facts` packet (`pixel scope-task --read-only`) carries `facts.relevance` (algorithm version 2): per task keyword, how many files contain it, define a symbol named with it or carry it in a path, and the co-files they meet in, weighted by how rare each word is. Its caps reach the response's `epistemics` and `warnings`. `relevance_only: true` returns the block without ranking targets.
