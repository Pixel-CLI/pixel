**evidence brief:** a brief larger than one hook may inject now reaches Claude Code and Codex. Claude Code caps a `UserPromptSubmit` hook's `additionalContext` at 10,000 characters and Codex spills past 2,500 estimated tokens, each per hook, so `pixel install` registers `brief_parts` hooks (default 3, 1 to 8 in `config.yaml`; 1 keeps the single hook): `run-hook task-event --event prompt-submit --part K/N`. Part 1 computes the brief and stores it under `.pixel/brief-parts/` (mode 0600, pruned after an hour); the others print their slice of at most 9,800 characters, cut at line boundaries, or nothing when part 1 does not answer within 2 s. `pixel doctor` names a missing part. Prompt-submit hook commands end in `|| true` and `run-hook` exits 0 on arguments it cannot parse, so an older binary never blocks a prompt with exit 2. Gemini, Antigravity and Devin keep one hook.
